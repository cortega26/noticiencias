# Noticiencias Strategic Operating System v1

**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Classification:** owner-approved strategic direction / periodically reviewable hypothesis set
**Scope:** product, audience, distribution and monetization
**Not a funding event:** no external spend, deployment, billing change or irreversible action is authorized here.

## Thesis

Noticiencias should become the **signal filter for science in Spanish**: a serious editorial product that helps readers understand what happened, how strong the evidence is, what is being exaggerated, and what remains unknown.

The operating flywheel is:

authority -> recurrent audience -> owned distribution -> monetization -> reinvestment

Technology and agents are leverage behind the product, not the external value proposition.

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- Scientific/editorial quality is a constraint, not something traded for traffic or revenue.
- No fabricated DOI, sources, reviewers, sample sizes, evidence strength or conclusions.
- Google/Discover/News are acquisition channels, not the business model.
- AdSense participation is **not** treated as a ranking signal or Google News advantage.
- Ads stay visually and operationally separate from evidence, sources and editorial conclusions.
- No generic Evidence API, full knowledge graph or B2B platform without demonstrated demand.
- No volume scaling while quality gates are failing.

## North Star

**Returning Engaged Readers — 28 days (RER28).**

A reader counts when they return within 28 days and perform a meaningful engagement event defined by the analytics contract, such as substantial read depth, second-article read, primary-source click or newsletter interaction.

## Scorecard

### Authority / quality

- primary-source coverage where a primary source exists
- evidence-type completeness where relevant
- editorial QA pass rate
- material correction rate and transparency

### Audience

- engaged sessions
- second-article rate
- 7-day and 28-day return
- branded/direct share

### Distribution

- Search, Google News and Discover
- generative/AI visibility where measurable
- referral and social

### Owned audience

- newsletter signup conversion
- active subscribers
- newsletter click rate
- unsubscribe rate

### Revenue

- total revenue
- Page RPM
- revenue per 1,000 engaged sessions
- revenue per returning reader
- sponsor/member revenue when introduced

### Efficiency

- API/model cost per published article
- human minutes per article
- source-to-publish time
- failure/rework rate

## Initial quality gates

Provisional thresholds; recalibrate only with evidence and record the decision:

- primary source present when identifiable: >=95%
- evidence/study type present when relevant: >=95%
- fabricated sources/metadata: 0
- materially unsupported claims: 0 target
- undisclosed substantive corrections: 0

A material quality-gate failure blocks volume expansion.

## Monetization policy

### AdSense — immediate controlled experiment

Use AdSense to learn real unit economics, not as an SEO/Google News tactic.

Measure before/after:

- Page RPM and revenue per 1,000 engaged sessions
- Core Web Vitals
- read completion and second-article rate
- newsletter conversion
- return rate

Initial guardrail: if ads cause a sustained roughly 10%+ deterioration in high-value engagement, or materially degrade Core Web Vitals, reduce density/placement even if RPM rises. Recalibrate after baseline variance is known.

### Later monetization ladder

1. AdSense / lightweight programmatic learning.
2. Direct newsletter or section sponsorship with explicit editorial firewall.
3. Voluntary support/membership without making core science coverage inaccessible.
4. Grants / institutional support where independence can be preserved.
5. B2B only after repeated customer-discovery evidence identifies a real problem.

## Editorial portfolio — initial allocation

Starting hypothesis, not permanent quota:

- 60% selected science news — acquisition/freshness
- 20% State of the Evidence — authority/evergreen/backlinks/return
- 10% Paper vs. Hype — brand/distribution/shareability
- 10% experiments

Do not overproduce to satisfy percentages. Quality and signal selection win.

## Product experiments

### Evidence Layer / Evidence Passport v1

A feature, not a moat. Surface source, evidence type, sample/context, main claim, limitation and known/unknown where available.

### Internal Science Radar v0

Cheap internal leverage for discovery, normalization, dedupe and prioritization. Do not build a generic research platform.

### State of the Evidence

Start with 3 living reference pages. Expand only if they outperform on backlinks, search longevity, return and/or newsletter conversion.

### Paper vs. Hype

Start with 5–10 items. Measure shares, saves, earned reach, backlinks and subscriber conversion. Kill or redesign if it does not create differentiated distribution.

### Newsletter

Treat it as a product: a curated weekly signal briefing, not a dump of recent links.

## Review cadence

### Weekly tactical review

Review publishing output, distribution spikes, newsletter, revenue and quality/pipeline failures. Do not make strategy changes from one noisy week.

### Monthly business review

Review audience, authority, owned audience, distribution, revenue and efficiency. Reallocate effort only with evidence.

### Quarterly thesis review

Ask whether the core thesis and product mix are still supported. Decide what to scale, hold, redesign or kill.

### Event-driven review triggers

- Search/Discover change >30% sustained for about 2 weeks -> strategy incident review.
- Format >=30% above relevant baseline across >=10 comparable pieces -> scale candidate.
- Persistent >20% below baseline -> reduce/rework/kill.
- Material ad engagement/CWV harm -> reduce/reconfigure.
- Quality gate failure -> freeze volume scaling.

These thresholds are initial operating hypotheses, not immutable policy.

## Capital allocation discipline

Use 70 / 20 / 10:

- 70% proven/core product
- 20% adjacent improvements
- 10% experiments

Any large initiative must state hypothesis, expected signal, cheapest valid experiment, measurement window, success threshold and kill threshold before scale.

Agents reduce implementation cost; they do not eliminate opportunity cost.

## 90-day execution gate

Sequence:

1. finish current UX/editorial hardening and measurement foundation
2. establish baseline scorecard
3. deploy controlled AdSense experiment
4. Evidence Layer v1
5. newsletter product v1
6. internal Radar v0
7. 3 State-of-the-Evidence pages
8. 5–10 Paper-vs-Hype experiments
9. 90-day review

At the gate decide what creates returning readers, subscribers, authority/backlinks and revenue without harming trust.

Do **not** build a generic Evidence Graph/API/B2B product before this gate unless external demand independently provides strong evidence.

## Strategic risk register

1. Indifference: respected but not habit-forming.
2. Google dependency.
3. Technical overbuilding.
4. Quality erosion from scale.
5. Weak owned distribution.
6. Advertising Goodhart.
7. False moat: copyable features mistaken for defensibility.

The defensible asset, if earned, is the compound of brand trust, editorial taste, recurrent audience, distribution, relationships and a proprietary historical corpus of structured editorial judgments/evidence updates.

## Current strategic position

No major product pivot is authorized by this document. The short-term move is an **adjustment of thesis and measurement discipline** while current UX/editorial improvements continue. Future direction must be earned by measured signals.
